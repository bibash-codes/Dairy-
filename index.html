Our Roots

Origin & About Gautam Dairy

Rooted in village tradition, empowered by technical expertise.

Pure Village Heart, Technical Mindset

Nestled in the tranquil village of Harkapur, Manebhanjyang 7, Okhaldhunga, Gautam Dairy was established with a clear mission: to bring fresh, natural, chemical-free dairy products directly from local village farms to your dining table.

Our cattle graze freely in lush mountain pastures, giving our milk and curd a uniquely natural taste, rich texture, and wholesome nourishment that factory-processed brands simply cannot replicate.
